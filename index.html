Value Investor

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Overview

Value Investor is a stock analysis application for Android that helps users evaluate ASX and US equities using Warren Buffett and Benjamin Graham value investing principles. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

Data We Do Not Collect

Value Investor does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following on external servers:

  • Your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information
  • Your account credentials or login details
  • Your location or device location data
  • Your camera, microphone, or contact list data
  • Your browsing history or in-app behaviour analytics

The app does not require an account, login, or registration of any kind.

Two device-level identifiers are handled by Google services within the app: an advertising identifier used by Google AdMob, and a push notification token used by Firebase Cloud Messaging. Neither identifies you personally, and neither is collected or stored by the developer. Both are described under Third-Party Services below.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Cached financial data (stock prices, ratios, scores) fetched from third-party APIs
  • Your watchlist (tickers and company names you have bookmarked)
  • Your disclaimer acceptance preference
  • Index and sector data refreshed periodically from our cloud data cache

This data remains entirely on your device and can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

Third-Party Services

Value Investor uses the following third-party services. Each service operates under its own privacy policy.

Google AdMob
Banner advertisements are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID) and other anonymous usage signals to deliver relevant ads. You can opt out of personalised advertising in your device settings under Privacy → Ads.
Google Privacy Policy

Yahoo Finance
Financial data (stock quotes, historical prices, financial statements) is fetched directly from Yahoo Finance public APIs. No personal data is sent to Yahoo Finance. Requests are made using standard HTTP headers. Yahoo Finance's data is used in accordance with their terms of service.
Yahoo Privacy Policy

Financial Modeling Prep (FMP)
US index quotes and supplementary financial ratios are sourced from the Financial Modeling Prep API. No personal data is transmitted. API calls contain only stock ticker symbols.
FMP Privacy Policy

Cloudflare R2 (Data Cache)
Pre-computed sector rankings and index data are downloaded from a Cloudflare R2 bucket. These are read-only downloads; no personal data is uploaded.

Firebase Cloud Messaging (Push Notifications)
The app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging, a Google service, to send occasional notifications telling you that refreshed market data is available. To deliver these, Firebase registers your device with Google and assigns it a registration token — a random identifier for the app installation on your device. This token is held by Google, is not linked to your name or any account, and is not collected, stored, or read by the developer. It is reset if you reinstall the app or clear its data. Notifications are optional: you may decline the notification permission when prompted, or turn notifications off at any time in your device settings, and the app will continue to work normally.
Firebase Privacy & Security

Permissions

Value Investor requests the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET — Required to fetch financial data from third-party APIs and display banner ads.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Used to check connectivity before making API requests.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — Allows Google AdMob to access the Android Advertising ID in order to serve ads. You can reset or delete this identifier at any time in your device settings.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Allows the app to show notifications when refreshed market data is available. You are asked for this permission, and may decline it without affecting any other feature.

No other permissions are requested. The app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage, or phone state.

Children's Privacy

Value Investor is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. Google AdMob ads served within the app are not configured for child-directed treatment.

Data Security

The developer operates no user accounts and no server that stores personal data, so there is no store of personal information at risk of a server-side breach. All locally cached financial data on your device is protected by Android's standard application sandbox security model. The advertising and push notification identifiers described above are handled by Google under its own security and privacy practices.

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland

If you use Value Investor in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you are shown a consent message the first time the app runs, before any advertisement is requested. It asks whether you agree to your data being used for personalised advertising and related purposes, and lets you consent, refuse, or choose individual purposes and vendors under Manage options.

Your choice is recorded on your device using the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework and is passed to Google AdMob and its advertising partners. Refusing does not restrict any feature of the app; you will simply see non-personalised or limited advertising instead.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time by opening Ad Privacy Settings at the bottom of the app's Home screen, which reopens the same consent options. This link is shown only where these rules apply.

The consent message is provided by Google's Consent Management Platform. Google acts as a data controller for the advertising data it processes; see the Google Privacy Policy and Google's list of advertising partners.
