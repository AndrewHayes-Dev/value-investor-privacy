Value Investor

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Overview

Value Investor is a stock analysis application for Android that helps users evaluate ASX and US equities using Warren Buffett and Benjamin Graham value investing principles. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

Data We Do Not Collect

The developer does not collect, receive, or store any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information
  • Account credentials or login details — the app has no accounts
  • GPS or precise device location
  • Camera, microphone, photo, file, or contact list data
  • Your browsing history, or analytics about how you use the app — the developer runs no analytics of its own

The app does not require an account, login, or registration of any kind.

Some data does leave your device through third-party services built into the app, each described under Third-Party Services below:

  • Google AdMob receives your device's advertising identifier, its IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), and information about ad and app interactions, in order to serve ads.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging holds a push notification token for your device.
  • TradingView serves the interactive price charts and receives your device's IP address and basic device information when a chart loads.
  • Cloud Firestore stores a feedback message, only if you choose to send one, with no identifier attached.

The advertising identifier, notification token and chart data are handled by Google and TradingView; none of them is collected or stored by the developer.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Cached financial data (stock prices, ratios, scores) fetched from third-party APIs
  • Your watchlist (tickers and company names you have bookmarked)
  • Your disclaimer acceptance preference
  • Index and sector data refreshed periodically from our cloud data cache

This data is never sent to the developer and can be cleared by uninstalling the app. If Android's own backup is turned on for your Google account, your watchlist and settings may be included in that backup so they can be restored on a new device; that backup is managed by Google under your account, and the developer cannot access it. Your acceptance of the disclaimer is deliberately excluded, so a restored or transferred install always asks you to accept it again.

Third-Party Services

Value Investor uses the following third-party services. Each service operates under its own privacy policy.

Google AdMob
The app shows banner ads, a full-screen ad when you return to the app, and an occasional full-screen ad after a data refresh, all served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google: your device's advertising ID and app set ID; your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate its general location; interactions such as app launches, taps and ad views; and diagnostic information such as app launch time and performance. This data is encrypted in transit. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads).
Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from apps that use its services

TradingView (Price Charts)
The interactive price charts on stock screens and in the Chart Guide are TradingView widgets, loaded from TradingView's servers when you open a chart. Loading a chart sends TradingView your device's IP address and basic device and browser information, and TradingView may use cookies and similar technologies for security, analytics and advertising, as described in its own privacy policy. The app passes TradingView only the ticker symbol being charted — no personal information.
TradingView Privacy Policy

Yahoo Finance (via RapidAPI)
Most financial data (stock quotes, historical prices, financial statements) comes from a Yahoo Finance dataset hosted on RapidAPI. It is fetched by the developer's weekly data pipeline, not by the app on your device, and reaches the app through the data cache described below. Your device never contacts RapidAPI or Yahoo Finance, so no data about you is sent to either.
RapidAPI Privacy Policy · Yahoo Privacy Policy

Financial Modeling Prep (FMP)
Supplementary financial data is sourced from the Financial Modeling Prep API by the developer's weekly data pipeline. As with Yahoo Finance, your device never contacts FMP, and the pipeline's requests contain only stock ticker symbols.
FMP Privacy Policy

Cloudflare R2 (Data Cache)
Pre-computed sector rankings and index data are downloaded from a Cloudflare R2 bucket. These are read-only downloads; no personal data is uploaded.

Cloud Firestore (Feedback Form)
If — and only if — you choose to use the Send Feedback screen, the message you write is stored in Cloud Firestore, a Google database service, so the developer can read it. Each submission records only the text you type (up to 500 characters), the category you pick (General, Bug Report or Feature Request), whether the device is Android or iOS, and the time of submission. No name, email address, account or device identifier is attached, and the app has no sign-in of any kind, so submissions cannot be traced back to you or linked to each other. Because the message is free text, please do not include personal details you would rather not share. Sending feedback is entirely optional and no other part of the app depends on it.
Firebase Privacy & Security

Firebase Cloud Messaging (Push Notifications)
The app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging, a Google service, to send occasional notifications telling you that refreshed market data is available. To deliver these, Firebase registers your device with Google and assigns it a registration token — a random identifier for the app installation on your device. This token is held by Google, is not linked to your name or any account, and is not collected, stored, or read by the developer. It is reset if you reinstall the app or clear its data. Notifications are optional: you may decline the notification permission when prompted, or turn notifications off at any time in your device settings, and the app will continue to work normally.
Firebase Privacy & Security

Permissions

Value Investor requests the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET — Required to fetch financial data from third-party APIs and display banner ads.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Used to check connectivity before making API requests.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — Allows Google AdMob to access the Android Advertising ID in order to serve ads. You can reset or delete this identifier at any time in your device settings.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Allows the app to show notifications when refreshed market data is available. You are asked for this permission, and may decline it without affecting any other feature.

The Google libraries built into the app also add the following permissions automatically. None of them is shown to you as a prompt, and none gives access to personal content:

  • ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS — Added by Google AdMob for Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising features, which you can control in your device's privacy settings.
  • com.google.android.c2dm.permission.RECEIVE — Lets Firebase Cloud Messaging deliver notifications.
  • WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, VIBRATE — Let Android's background-work and notification libraries finish short tasks and vibrate for a notification.

The app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, files, or phone state.

Children's Privacy

Value Investor is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Google AdMob ads served within the app are not configured for child-directed treatment.

Data Security

The developer operates no user accounts, and the only information the app ever sends to a developer-controlled store is a feedback message you choose to submit, which is held in Cloud Firestore and carries no identifying information. There is no database of user profiles, contact details or credentials to be breached. All locally cached financial data on your device is protected by Android's standard application sandbox security model. The advertising, notification and chart data described above is handled by Google and TradingView under their own security and privacy practices.

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland

If you use Value Investor in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you are shown a consent message the first time the app runs, before any advertisement is requested. It asks whether you agree to your data being used for personalised advertising and related purposes, and lets you consent, refuse, or choose individual purposes and vendors under Manage options.

Your choice is recorded on your device using the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework and is passed to Google AdMob and its advertising partners. Refusing does not restrict any feature of the app; you will simply see non-personalised or limited advertising instead.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time by opening Ad Privacy Settings at the bottom of the app's Home screen, which reopens the same consent options. This link is shown only where these rules apply.

The consent message is provided by Google's Consent Management Platform. Google acts as a data controller for the advertising data it processes; see the Google Privacy Policy and Google's list of advertising partners.
